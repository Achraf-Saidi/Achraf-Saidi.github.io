About me.

I am Achraf Saidi, a PhD researcher and Teaching & Research Assistant at UCLouvain, within ISBA and LIDAM. I am also co-founder and CTO of NovalisAI.

My background combines software engineering, data science and statistics. That combination is still how I approach most problems: understand the statistical structure, work carefully with the data, and build the tools needed to turn the analysis into something usable.

My research interests include calibration, survival analysis, machine learning and deep learning, with a growing focus on biomedical and high-dimensional data. Outside academia, I have worked on energy optimization, industrial software, public-sector NLP and enterprise systems.

Beyond the university, I co-founded NovalisAI, where I serve as Chief Technology Officer. I am also an associate of Jet Things and Jet Next, contributing to practical software, data and digital-transformation projects.
