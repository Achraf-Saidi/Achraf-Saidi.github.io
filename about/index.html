A bit about me.

I am Achraf Saidi, a PhD researcher and Teaching & Research Assistant at UCLouvain, within ISBA and LIDAM.

I come from a data-science background and I like working between statistics and computer science. My main interests are machine learning, deep learning, calibration, survival analysis and new AI technologies.

Alongside research, I am co-founder and CTO of NovalisAI. I am also an associate of Jet Things and Jet Next in Algeria, where we are interested in helping companies digitize their processes and adopt practical software and AI tools.

I am naturally curious and I read a lot—sometimes directly related to my research, sometimes not at all. AI, mathematics, technology, science and history are the subjects I return to most often.
