FAQ

Is this an official ATS score?

No. ATS products use different ranking rules. This is a transparent heuristic diagnostic designed to help you tailor a CV, not predict a recruiter’s exact score.

Do you store my CV?

No. The analysis is performed locally in your browser. This site has no CV upload endpoint and no account system.

Should I copy every missing keyword?

No. Only use terminology that truthfully reflects your skills and experience. Keyword stuffing can make a CV worse.
