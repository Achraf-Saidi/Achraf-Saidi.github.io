# BILADI — audit and first encyclopedia expansion

Baseline inspected: commit 739f9dc, October 2026. Scope restricted to `biladi/` inside the existing personal GitHub Pages repository. Other projects are not changed.

## What was already real

Buildless ES modules, hash routes, a 69-wilaya SVG atlas, 76 credited local photographs, 26 multilingual articles, six culture profiles, eight short person profiles, an open inventory of 4,348 tourism entries plus 36 airports, browser-local favourites and trips, editable budget assumptions and calendar exports. Existing checks pass across 2,688 journey combinations and 179 DOM page checks. The live HTML and app resource respond successfully over HTTPS.

## Main shortcomings

Most secondary articles were short and had no sections. People appeared mainly as monogram teasers. Wilaya pages offered a photograph and directory rather than a local editorial dossier. Article reading had no table of contents, explicit related-knowledge navigation or simplified mode. Photo details displayed one image and credits with no sequence or zoom. Directory details relied on external map searches; the large available location inventory was not integrated into a map exploration experience. Historical navigation used a short strip of article dates. New material must not imply translations, live data or a completed encyclopedic corpus it does not contain.

## Changes

- Authored 23 new dossiers, approximately 7,240 words before summaries and references, with section-level sources, explicit uncertainty and twelve individual biographies.
- Added 69 explicit knowledge relations, four ordered discovery trails and 24 chronology milestones with period filters.
- Added a domain/search encyclopedia page, article contents, full and simplified reading modes and related articles.
- Added fourteen individually authored territorial dossiers, actual dataset facts for all wilayas and direct integrated-map access.
- Added an SVG map explorer using the retained geographic projection and open inventory. Filters, region selection, local point details, zoom, pan and keyboard-accessible points work without external map tiles. It is a simplified location overview, not a road-routing engine. First 120 matching points are shown with an explicit refinement notice.
- Added a photographic collection route and full-viewport lightbox with touch swipe, keyboard navigation, zoom, captions and credits.
- Preserved the original planner and existing multilingual content. The new deep dossiers are currently French; availability is explicit in other UI languages.
- Replaced the footer name with Achraf Saidi.

## Editorial and verification limits

This is an implemented expansion, not the promised completion of all knowledge about Algeria. Eleven archive portraits/group photographs have been added. Four architectural photographs have also been added: the Palais des Raïs (two views), the Sidi Rached bridge and the Ahmed Bey palace. The catalogue now holds 91 authentic images with file-level credits and licences. Some historical facts are documented by collective secondary sources alongside institutional references; further archival and scholarly review is still needed. Sources are linked at section level, but that does not imply exhaustive critical edition work.

The 69-wilaya division is verified against the official law and decree. Current map limits derive from simplified open data; they are not legally authoritative boundaries. New territorial texts cover fourteen wilayas, while all 69 retain functional pages. Contemporary political history, many regional cultures, all remaining deep wilaya dossiers, a broader natural history and full translations remain editorial work.

No supported browser-control skill is available in this environment. Tests simulate the DOM; responsive CSS is implemented, but desktop/mobile visual rendering, actual touch gestures and screen reader experience have not been verified on a browser/device. Hash-routed articles retain the existing search-engine indexing limitation. No claim of a world-reference encyclopedia or exemplary device validation is made at this stage.

This is DOM simulation, not visual browser verification.
