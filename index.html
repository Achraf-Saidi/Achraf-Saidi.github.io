Research focus

Reliable models for complex data.

I am interested in models that are not only accurate, but whose predictions can be interpreted, evaluated and trusted in settings where the data are incomplete, censored, high-dimensional or clinically complex.

01

Calibration & predictive reliability

How well predicted risks, probabilities and scores correspond to what is eventually observed, and how this reliability can be assessed in practice.

02

Survival & event-time data

Statistical learning for time-to-event outcomes, including the complications created by censoring and incomplete observation.

03

Machine learning & deep learning

Modern predictive models, representation learning and the statistical questions that arise when flexible models are used on real data.

04

Biomedical & multi-omics data

Applications to high-dimensional biological, clinical and imaging data, with an interest in methods that remain useful beyond benchmark performance.
