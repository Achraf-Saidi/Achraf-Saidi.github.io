Current focus

Research that stays reliable when reality changes.

My current research direction focuses on trustworthy predictive modelling under time, censoring and distribution shift, with a particular interest in calibration, sequential monitoring and medical applications.

01 · Calibration

Reliable predictive probabilities

Studying whether predictive models remain calibrated as populations, environments and data-generating mechanisms evolve.

02 · Sequential inference

Monitoring over time

Developing statistically valid ways to detect miscalibration or degradation repeatedly, rather than only in a one-shot evaluation.

03 · Survival & medicine

Censored outcomes

Connecting sequential validation to time-to-event settings where outcomes may be delayed or censored and naive monitoring can be misleading.
