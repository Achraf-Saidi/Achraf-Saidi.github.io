01 / Research

Research interests

Statistical methodology for predictions that remain meaningful outside ideal data conditions.

Research overview ↗

Calibration & predictive reliability

Assessing whether predicted probabilities and risks agree with observed outcomes, and studying how that agreement can be monitored over time.

Survival analysis & censoring

Learning and evaluating time-to-event predictions when follow-up is incomplete, including the statistical challenges created by right censoring.

Machine learning for biomedical data

Connecting flexible predictive models with careful validation, interpretable evaluation and applications to clinical and high-dimensional data.
