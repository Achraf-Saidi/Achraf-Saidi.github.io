## By Achraf Saidi

Achraf Notebooks is an independent, free, open-source learning library for mathematics, statistics, programming and artificial intelligence. Its method connects intuition, definitions, derivations, experiments, code and critical thinking. It does not award accredited degrees.

## Available now

This first edition contains **12 original lessons in French and English**, five mathematical demonstrations, explained practice questions, an orientation diagnostic, full-page search, a glossary, two coding labs and four downloadable projects or experiments. Iris and Wine projects use real datasets; gradient and backpropagation experiments are synthetic.

The catalogue’s **136 modules** are a roadmap. An initial derivative notebook does not mean the full analysis course is finished. Published lessons do not claim independent scientific review. Automated checks cover builds, internal links and selected calculations; they cannot alone establish educational quality.

## Still to build

Complete frequentist and Bayesian statistics pathways, SQL/NoSQL, advanced deep learning, RL, LLMs and the real-cohort multi-omics project remain in development plans. Current practice questions support single-choice and numerical answers; matching, ordering and multiple selections are future work. Guaranteed offline access and cross-device synchronization are not implemented.

Accessibility practices include semantic structure, keyboard access, text accompanying graphs and reduced-motion support. No WCAG certification or unmeasured performance score is claimed.

## Contributing

The [source](https://github.com/Achraf-Saidi/Achraf-Saidi.github.io/tree/main/achraf-notebooks) includes contribution guidance, curriculum manifests, references and verification protocols. Corrections should identify the notebook and passage, explain the reasoning and cite a primary source where needed. New lessons need equivalent translations, checked examples and explained solutions.

Editorial statuses distinguish **planned**, **in development**, **draft**, **technically validated**, **scientifically reviewed** and **published**. Publication and review are separate attributes. A scientific review must record the reviewer, date and scope.

## Licences and personal data

Original code: **MIT**. Original lessons and exercises: **CC BY 4.0**, attributing Achraf Saidi and identifying changes. Third-party works and datasets retain their own licences.

No account is required. Progress, answers and notes stay in your browser and can be exported and imported; clearing browser data removes them. Code entered in labs runs in a local browser runtime. Voluntarily loading Python or R contacts their distribution servers. GitHub Pages handles hosting requests under its own conditions.

The [implementation report](https://github.com/Achraf-Saidi/Achraf-Saidi.github.io/tree/main/achraf-notebooks/source/docs) records executed checks and the limits of this edition.
