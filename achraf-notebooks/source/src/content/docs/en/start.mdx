<Diagnostic lang="en" />

## A useful study rhythm

Read each notebook once without opening its solutions. Work through calculations on paper, change an interactive demonstration and explain what happened aloud. Then attempt the exercises. If notation blocks you, follow the exact prerequisite instead of memorizing a formula you cannot explain.

Displayed times cover an initial reading and exploration; mastery may require several sessions. Revisiting foundations is expected.

## Three entry points

- **From zero:** [numbers and fractions](/achraf-notebooks/en/foundations/numbers/), followed by algebra, functions, limits and derivatives.
- **Towards ML:** matrices, Python or R, probability, then [regression](/achraf-notebooks/en/learning/regression/).
- **Towards biology:** [multi-omics introduction](/achraf-notebooks/en/research/multiomics/), then its prerequisites and school K’s roadmap.

Use [My notebook](/achraf-notebooks/en/notebook/) to record and export progress.
