## Why begin with a fraction?

A study reports “18 positive responses among 60 participants”. Before fitting any statistical model, distinguish the **count**, 18, the **total**, 60, and the **proportion**, $18/60=3/10=0.3$. A percentage expresses that proportion per hundred: $30\%$. A proportion is not a count: doubling the group while preserving its proportion gives 36 positives among 120.

**Goal.** Calculate with signed numbers and fractions, interpret proportions, and read the symbols used in later notebooks. No prerequisites.

## One number, several representations

The natural numbers $\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ count objects. The integers $\mathbb Z$ include negative numbers. Rational numbers $\mathbb Q$ have the form $a/b$, where $a,b$ are integers and $b\ne0$. The real numbers $\mathbb R$ also include irrational numbers such as $\sqrt2$. Positions on the real number line represent these numbers.

A fraction $a/b$ means division, or $a$ pieces each of size $1/b$. Thus $3/4$ is three quarters of a unit. Multiplying numerator and denominator by the same nonzero number preserves the quantity: $3/4=6/8$. Addition requires equal-sized pieces:

$$\frac12+\frac13=\frac36+\frac26=\frac56.$$

Multiplication works directly: $(2/3)(3/5)=2/5$. Dividing by a nonzero fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal: $(1/2)/(3/4)=(1/2)(4/3)=2/3$. Check: two thirds of $3/4$ is indeed $1/2$.

## Signs and precedence

A minus sign can indicate a negative number or a subtraction. In $5-(-2)=7$, subtracting a negative quantity means adding its opposite. Two negative factors give a positive product; exactly one negative factor gives a negative product.

Evaluate parentheses first, then powers, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. Thus $2+3\times4=14$, whereas $(2+3)\times4=20$. For a positive integer $n$, the exponent in $a^n$ denotes repeated multiplication: $2^3=8$. For $a\ne0$, $a^0=1$ and $a^{-1}=1/a$. Note that $(-2)^2=4$, but $-2^2=-4$.

## Equality, approximation and units

The symbol $=$ means exact equality; $\approx$ means approximation. $1/3\approx0.333$, but $1/3\ne0.333$. The symbols $<$ and $\le$ mean “strictly less than” and “less than or equal to”. The expression $x\in\mathbb R$ means that $x$ is a real number.

The absolute value $|x|$ is the distance from $x$ to zero: $|-3|=3$. It measures a discrepancy without its sign. A prediction of 7 against an observation of 10 has signed error $7-10=-3$ and absolute error 3.

Quantities retain their units. Covering 120 km in 2 h gives average speed $(120\ \mathrm{km})/(2\ \mathrm h)=60\ \mathrm{km/h}$. Adding 3 metres to 2 seconds makes no dimensional sense. This discipline matters later for derivatives and matrices.

## A misleading percentage change

Start with 100, increase by 20%, then decrease by 20%. The first operation gives $100(1+0.2)=120$; the second gives $120(1-0.2)=96$. The percentages use different bases, so they do not cancel. Generally, a relative change $r$ maps a quantity $q$ to $q(1+r)$.

```python
positive = 18
total = 60
proportion = positive / total
print(proportion, 100 * proportion)  # 0.3 30.0
```

Here `/` divides and `*` multiplies. Computers usually represent real numbers approximately; floating-point arithmetic will matter in scientific calculations.

## Exercises and reasoning

1. Calculate $3/4-1/6$.
2. Accuracy rises from 80% to 88%. Find the change in percentage points and the relative change.
3. Explain why $1/(a+b)=1/a+1/b$ is not an identity.

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

Use a common denominator in 1. Distinguish a difference from a ratio in 2. A single counterexample answers 3.

</details>


<details>
<summary>Worked solutions</summary>



1. $3/4=9/12$ and $1/6=2/12$, so the difference is $7/12$.
2. The increase is 8 percentage points. The relative increase is $(88-80)/80=0.1=10\%$.
3. Set $a=b=1$. The left side is $1/2$, while the right side is 2. An identity must hold for every allowed value, so this is not one.



</details>
