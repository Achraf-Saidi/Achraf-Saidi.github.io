How I approach a project

Start with the problem, not the model.

Not every problem needs AI. I prefer to understand the decision, the data and the constraints first, then decide what level of analysis or technology actually makes sense.

01

Understand

Clarify the objective, current workflow, available data, constraints and what a useful outcome would look like.

02

Assess

Identify whether the problem calls for statistics, ML, optimization, software, process redesign — or something simpler.

03

Scope

Define a realistic first step, expected deliverables and the information needed to move forward.

04

Build

When there is a good fit, move from analysis to a prototype, model, tool or implementation that can actually be used.

What to include

A good first email can be very short.

Tell me who you are, what you are working on, the problem you are trying to solve, and any relevant deadline or constraint. If I am not the right person for it, I would rather say so quickly than force a solution.
