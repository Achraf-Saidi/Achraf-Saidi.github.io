Selected experience

Applied data, optimization & software.

My applied work spans energy systems, industrial software, public-sector data, biomedical machine learning and enterprise digitalization.

Most underlying data and implementation details are private. The descriptions below are intentionally limited to the parts that can be discussed publicly.

Luminus
Energy & optimization

BESS dispatch optimization

Worked on utility-scale battery storage in the context of electricity markets, combining market-data analysis with optimization. The work included day-ahead dispatch, cycle-budget decisions, performance metrics and the economic value of battery cycling. It became the basis of my master's thesis in Data Science Engineering.

Sonatrach
Industrial software

Large project-management application

Designed and developed a substantial project-management system around real operational requirements. The work combined software engineering, workflow design, project tracking and reporting in a large industrial environment.

Belgian Foreign Service
Applied NLP

Evidence-grounded narrative generation

Contributed to a project for development-project reporting that combined information retrieval, TF-IDF and classical NLP with local language models. The objective was to generate, check and complete narrative information from existing evidence while reducing unsupported output.

Biomedical data
ML & deep learning

Predictive modelling and medical imaging

Worked on academic projects involving cancer and healthcare data, predictive modelling, semantic image segmentation and representation-learning experiments. These projects strengthened my interest in biomedical applications of statistics and machine learning.

Enterprise systems

Built ERP-style business software and operational tools for real workflows, including management, reporting and time-tracking needs. I am particularly interested in the point where software design, process understanding and data meet.
