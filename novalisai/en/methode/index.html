What we build

  1. 01

    An hour, together. A free, no-obligation conversation about your company, needs and initial constraints. What you receive : An initial understanding of the need

  2. 02

    Understand the ground. Observe operations, tools and handovers. Separate the cause from its symptoms. What you receive : A diagnosis of workflows and priorities

  3. 03

    Design the right response. Compare options, design journeys and test assumptions. Assess automation and AI where they can help. What you receive : Journeys and a first prototype

  4. 04

    Set the framework. Define features, scope, deliverables, schedule and budget before implementation. What you receive : A specification and proposal

  5. 05

    Build through milestones. Develop, test and show each version. Feedback and approvals remain visible in Novalis OS. What you receive : Progressive, verifiable deliveries

  6. 06

    Support real use. Prepare launch, help users adopt the product and define maintenance and evolution. What you receive : A deployment and support plan
