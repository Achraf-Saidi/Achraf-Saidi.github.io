NOVALIS / Our method

Clear steps. Shared decisions.

Eight stages make decisions, deliverables and approvals understandable. Scope and commitments are defined in your project proposal.

What we build

  1. 01

    Discovery — Understand the work, users and time-consuming difficulties. Identify constraints and assumptions to check. What you receive : Discovery summary. The decision to make together : Which problem comes first?

  2. 02

    Specification — Define journeys, business rules, access, integrations and acceptance criteria. Prioritise a useful initial scope. What you receive : Appropriate specification. The decision to make together : What does an acceptable result look like?

  3. 03

    Design — Design interfaces, structure data and APIs, and examine architecture and security choices. What you receive : Prototype and architecture. The decision to make together : Does the journey match real work?

  4. 04

    Proposal — Define increments, dependencies, responsibilities, milestones, budget and change conditions. What you receive : Proposal and plan. The decision to make together : Which increment, budget and milestones?

  5. 05

    Development — Build testable increments, review code and demonstrate them with your users. What you receive : Working increments. The decision to make together : What needs to change after the demonstration?

  6. 06

    Acceptance — Check business rules, integrations, permissions, performance and accessible journeys. Resolve agreed acceptance issues. What you receive : Acceptance and corrections. The decision to make together : Have the agreed criteria been met?

  7. 07

    Deployment — Prepare infrastructure, migration, backups, documentation and user onboarding. What you receive : Solution and documentation. The decision to make together : Who supports the launch?

  8. 08

    Evolution — Organise support, maintenance, updates and improvements under the agreed contract. What you receive : Support plan. The decision to make together : Which improvement adds the most value?

NOVALIS / DELIVERY

A clear scope before development.

We define objectives, users, priorities and acceptance criteria. The proposal distinguishes initial features, integrations and potential extensions. You keep a clear view of the scope and decisions.
