Before takingthe first step.

What if we do not yet know what to build?＋

That is the point of the first conversation and diagnosis: understand the problem before defining a tool.

Can we keep our current software?＋

We assess possible connections, access and constraints. Integration is confirmed after technical verification.

How is the budget defined?＋

After scoping, a proposal sets out the scope, deliverables, milestones and conditions. An automatic price cannot replace this work.

Is AI always necessary?＋

No. A clear rule, an API or a better interface may be enough. We assess AI where its contribution can be verified.
