NOVALIS / ANALYSE

Costs move from €1,410 to €1,530. Transport accounts for €90 of the €120 change. Compare allocations before making a decision.

↳Source: the data and rules in this demonstration.
Model and limitations

A: €7/unit, capacity 120. B: €4/unit, adjustable capacity, €60 fixed charge if used. Units are integers and all demand must be fulfilled. Other costs are fixed at €830. Every possible allocation is compared; transport time and risk are outside this model.

The projection is the arithmetic mean of the last three observed months. It is not a trained forecast and has no uncertainty interval.
