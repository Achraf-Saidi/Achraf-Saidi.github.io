Teaching

Making quantitative ideas usable.

As a Teaching & Research Assistant at UCLouvain, I teach and support students in statistics, programming and quantitative methods.

My teaching style is built around the same principle I use in research: understand what each step is doing, rather than memorize a sequence of commands.

Core statistical reasoning, model interpretation, exercises and practical data analysis.
