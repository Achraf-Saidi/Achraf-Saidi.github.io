Teaching

Statistics taught from first principles.

As a Teaching & Research Assistant at UCLouvain, I contribute to statistics education and practical training. Public material will be added here course by course when it can be shared.

Teaching philosophy

I aim to connect formal statistical reasoning with computation, intuition and reproducible examples, so that students understand not only how to apply a method but why it works.

Public resources

Future entries will include selected notes, R/Python examples, exercises and methodological explanations. Restricted course material and assessment solutions will not be published.
